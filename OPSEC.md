# OPSEC

This repo is public and intentionally sanitized. It is designed to share methodology and defensive learnings without exposing sensitive operational details.

## Excluded From Public
- secrets, tokens, credentials, or private keys
- target data, client details, or identifiable infrastructure
- step-by-step offensive runbooks or exploitation instructions
- live run state, logs, loot, or raw engagement notes

## Safe Reproduction
- Use sanitized examples and placeholders (for example, `<TARGET_IP>`).
- Test only against systems you own or are explicitly authorized to assess.
- Keep operational notes in `local/` (gitignored) or another private store.
- Redact outputs before sharing or publishing.

## Reporting
If you find sensitive data in this repo, follow the process in `SECURITY.md`.
